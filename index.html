About Me

I'm Ashianti Summer, a Graphic Designer specializing in branding, social media campaigns, creative marketing visuals, and digital design experiences.

I help businesses build a strong visual identity through modern, strategic, and impactful creative solutions.

As a UI/UX Designer, I focus on creating clean, user-friendly, and purposeful digital experiences. I enjoy designing interfaces that are easy to navigate, visually balanced, and built around what users need, whether it is a website, mobile app, dashboard, or digital platform.

My design process combines creativity, research, and attention to detail. I work with layouts, typography, color, spacing, and visual hierarchy to make every design feel clear, professional, and engaging from the first interaction.

As a Graphic Designer, I create branding materials, pubmats, campaign visuals, social media content, email designs, and marketing graphics that help brands communicate with confidence. I aim to make designs that are not only attractive, but also strategic and meaningful.

I am passionate about turning ideas into visuals that connect with people. Whether I am designing a brand identity or building a digital interface, my goal is to create work that feels modern, intentional, and memorable.

B&B (Browse and Borrow)

B&B, which stands for Browse and Borrow, is a mobile app I created for our research project. The app was designed to help students browse available books and borrow from our school library in a more convenient way.

Our library is located on the second floor of the admin building, so it is not always easy for students to access it quickly. This research project focused on making the borrowing process easier, more organized, and more accessible through a mobile solution.

I worked on this app for almost one year and I was the only one who created the app. Through this project, our research ranked 1st in the HUMSS Category, and we proceeded to present it at our School Division Office, where different schools were also present.

PowerKick

PowerKick is a soccer ball game app design and prototype that I created for our Physics project. The game challenges players to kick a soccer ball toward a target, earn points, and progress through levels from easy to more difficult.

The app connects gameplay with Physics concepts such as work, kinetic energy, calculated force, motion, and power. Through each level, players can experience how force and movement affect the ball while trying to hit the target accurately.

Players can also switch the ball across different levels, making the game feel more interactive as the difficulty increases. I designed the mobile app interface and created the Figma prototype for this current project that we are working on.
